FAQ

Questions people ask

Is it really hand-drawn? Not AI?

Yes. Every portrait is drawn by hand with graphite pencils. You get progress photos while I work, and you can watch full time-lapses on my YouTube channel.

What kind of photo should I send?

A clear photo taken in daylight, with the face sharp and not covered. Avoid heavy filters, dark rooms or tiny faces cropped from group photos.

Can you combine people from different photos?

Yes, usually. Send all the photos and I'll tell you whether they'll work together.

How do I pay?

Once we agree on the details, you pay by UPI. I start drawing after the advance is received.

How long does it take?

Usually 5–7 days to draw, plus shipping time. For a specific date, tell me when you order.
